SmartSalary — Terms of Use

SmartSalary is provided as a personal budgeting and record-keeping tool. It helps you estimate income, plan budgets, record expenses, and track savings goals.

Not a financial institution

SmartSalary is not a bank, wallet, payment processor, investment service, or financial adviser. It does not hold, receive, or transfer real money. Calculations are estimates and should be checked against your actual records.

Local storage and backups

Your information is saved in the browser on your device. You are responsible for keeping backup copies and protecting exported backup files.

Use at your own discretion

Review entries for accuracy. The app is provided without a guarantee that it will be suitable for every purpose.
