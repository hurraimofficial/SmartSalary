SmartSalary — Privacy Notice

SmartSalary is an offline personal budgeting tool. It does not require an account, phone number, password, Google sign-in, OTP, or internet connection for normal use.

Local data

Your salary, budget categories, expenses, savings goals, and related records are stored in your browser's local storage on this device. The app does not send this information to a server.

Backups

You can export a JSON backup file. That file may contain your personal budgeting information, so keep it somewhere private. Importing a backup replaces the app's current saved data.

Your responsibility

Clearing browser site data, uninstalling the browser, or losing the device may remove locally stored information. Export backups regularly. This app does not store or transfer real money and is not a bank or wallet.
